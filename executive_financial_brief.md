---

### 1. Executive Summary & Core Verdict
An extensive financial audit of **50,000+ General Ledger transactions** ($78.5M baseline revenue run-rate) across all seven corporate cost centers was synthesized into a dynamic sensitivity suite. Under our **Base Case Forecast**, the enterprise will generate **$91.45M Gross Revenue** with an **EBITDA of $17.38M (19.0% operating margin)**. 

However, multi-variable sensitivity modeling identifies a critical inflection boundary: if macroeconomic inflation and cloud compute expansion surpass **6.8%**, accompanied by top-line growth softening below **11.5%**, EBITDA margins compress by **460 bps**, exposing a **$4.2M capital deficit** against projected expansion targets.
